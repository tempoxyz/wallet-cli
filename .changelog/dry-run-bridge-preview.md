`tempo request --dry-run --funding auto` on an x402 or MPP `evm` offer the remote payer cannot cover now previews the bridge in `funding`: the shortfall, the route and source token the payment would choose, the quote's `amount_in`, fee, gas, and total, whether `--max-funding-spend` covers it, and every route's quote or why it declined. It quotes the same Relay and Stargate routes a payment would, and signs, saves, and bridges nothing.
