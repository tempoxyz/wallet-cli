Add `tempo wallet stocks` to trade tokenized US stocks (Dinari dShares) through Tempo Stocks: `list`, `quote`, `portfolio`, `orders`, `buy`, `sell` and `cancel`. The access key signs in; each order is approved with the account's passkey in the browser. `TEMPO_STOCKS_URL` sets the service, which defaults to its dev deployment.
